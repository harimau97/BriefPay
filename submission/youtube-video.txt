BriefPay: PayPal hold captured only after the brief passes

================================================================================
DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

BriefPay places a freelance budget on a PayPal sandbox authorization hold. Claude Haiku checks the delivery URL against the brief. The server captures the hold only after every criterion passes. A failed review leaves the hold in place. This is not licensed escrow.

Live demo: https://meganesoftware.cc/briefpay/

================================================================================
RECORDING
Win+Alt+R starts and stops the Windows recording. The file usually lands in Videos.
Open https://meganesoftware.cc/briefpay/
On the home page, open only the Landing page redesign whose badge is Draft and whose amount is USD 200.00.
Do not open the Captured or Voided jobs with the same title.
Use the United States Personal sandbox account in the sandbox.paypal.com window.
Do not use a live PayPal account or a Korean PayPal account.
================================================================================

1. Press Start as client. There is no password.
2. Open the Draft job. Press the yellow PayPal button and approve the $200 authorization.
3. Show the Authorized badge. Capture ID stays none. This is a hold, not a payout.
4. Switch the header to Freelancer. Press Accept job. The badge becomes In progress.
5. Press Fail example, then Submit delivery.
   The URL is https://meganesoftware.cc/briefpay/demo/fail
6. Press Run AI review. While the button says Verifying, do not refresh.
   The log names claude-haiku-5-5. This can take more than ten seconds.
7. Show the Revision badge, the failed rows, and their evidence.
   Capture ID is still none. Do not press Cancel job or Force payout.
8. Press Pass example, then Submit delivery.
   The URL is https://meganesoftware.cc/briefpay/demo/pass
9. Press Run AI review again. When every criterion passes, the server captures on that same click.
10. Show the Captured badge, the Capture ID, and the timeline. Stop.

If the red line under the PayPal button says PayPal returned a currency other than USD, stop and do not keep that take.
No receipt invoice yet still counts as a successful capture.
